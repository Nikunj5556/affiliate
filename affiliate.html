FAQ

Questions, answered.

Your commission = product price × your tier rate (20/30/40%). If you gave a coupon discount, that is deducted. Example: ₹500 product, Standard tier (20%), coupon gives 10% off → commission = (₹500 × 20%) − (₹500 × 10%) = ₹100 − ₹50 = ₹50.
Upgrades are based on cumulative sales. Growth Partner (30%) unlocks at 25 total sales. Elite Partner (40%) unlocks at 100 total sales. Your dashboard always shows your current progress toward the next tier.
Percentage discounts reduce the product price by a % (e.g., 15% off). Fixed discounts reduce by a flat INR amount (e.g., ₹200 off). Fixed discount coupons are only available to Growth and Elite partners, with a maximum of ₹498.
Your link format is: creatorstack.breakfastclub.co.in/PRODUCT_ID?ref=YOUR_ID. When a buyer clicks your link and makes a paid purchase, the sale is automatically attributed to your account.
Yes — create as many coupon codes as you want from your dashboard. Each can have different rates and validity settings. Your dashboard shows usage counts for each coupon so you can identify your best-performing codes.
Go to Payouts in your dashboard and click "Request Payout". Minimum amount is ₹100. Processing takes 3–5 business days to your registered Indian bank account.
When creating a coupon you choose: either a number-of-sales validity (e.g., first 50 uses) or a number-of-days validity (e.g., valid for 30 days). Once the limit is reached, the coupon becomes inactive automatically.
Anyone with an Indian bank account — students, freelancers, creators, self-employed individuals, or side-hustlers. Complete the onboarding form, verify your email via OTP, and you're live immediately as a Standard Partner.
Sales are tracked in real-time. The moment a buyer completes a paid order through your link or coupon, it appears in your dashboard and commission is added to your pending revenue balance.
Elite affiliates hitting 100+ monthly sales are enrolled in our exclusive rewards program — international trips, premium electronics, accessories, and monthly recognition. Gifts are dispatched unexpectedly, so just keep an eye on your inbox! 🎁

What an acquisition can include.

Each product has its own scope. Included assets, technical support, and handover terms are confirmed during the acquisition discussion.

Source Code

Complete application codebase where applicable to the specific product.

Product Interface

Existing polished UI, product patterns, and user workflows where included.

Documentation

Available technical, setup, and handover documentation for the product.

Deployment Assistance

Deployment assistance may be available where included in the agreed package.

Brand Assets

Existing brand and interface assets where they are part of the acquisition.

Handover

Transfer and onboarding support as agreed with the buyer and documented in terms.

Scope varies by product. Third-party service accounts, credentials, and subscriptions are handled separately unless explicitly included in an acquisition agreement.

Common questions

FAQ

Acquisition discussions are product-specific. These answers explain the general approach.

The scope depends on the individual project. Each product discussion covers the relevant software assets, source code where applicable, product interface, documentation, and agreed handover terms.

Many products are designed as extensible foundations and can be suitable for rebranding. The permitted branding and product rights are confirmed during the acquisition discussion and reflected in the final agreement.

Source code can be included where applicable, but it is always confirmed for the individual acquisition package. The relevant repository, configuration, and handover scope are discussed before a transaction.

Appropriate product information can be shared during a serious inquiry. The level of access, demonstrations, and technical review depend on the product and stage of the acquisition conversation.

Deployment assistance may be available as part of a specific acquisition package. Availability, environments, and the buyer's technical requirements are reviewed before confirming the scope.

Exclusivity is a term to be explicitly agreed for each acquisition. Do not assume a product is exclusively transferred until the acquisition terms say so.

Yes. Use the inquiry form to explain your intended use, requirements, and any requested scope. We can then determine whether a tailored acquisition or custom project arrangement is practical.

Third-party services, subscriptions, credentials, and provider accounts are generally separate unless explicitly stated in the agreed package. Buyers may need to configure their own accounts and credentials after handover.
